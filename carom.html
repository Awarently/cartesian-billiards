Ideal geometry. Cushions reflect at equal angles. After the first ball the cue ball follows the hit model: Roll uses the natural-roll rule (about 34 degrees off the aim line at a half-ball hit, the "30-degree rule"); Stun uses the tangent line (90 degrees to the line of centres). No side spin, throw, cushion compression or speed. Real tables play shorter or longer. Use the numbers as a starting reference.
